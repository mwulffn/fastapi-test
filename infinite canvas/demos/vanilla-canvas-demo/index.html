How to Use:

  • Click buttons to add nodes
  • Drag nodes to move them
  • Enable connect mode
  • Click two nodes to connect
  • Right-click to delete

⚠️ Limitations:

This vanilla implementation demonstrates the complexity of building from scratch:

  • No zoom/pan (would need complex transforms)
  • Basic drag & drop only
  • Simple connections (no bezier curves)
  • No edge routing
  • No undo/redo
  • No serialization
  • Limited styling

Building these features would take weeks vs hours with a library.
